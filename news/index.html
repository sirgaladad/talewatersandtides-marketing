The whole Arkansas River, and new limits on the White

Twelve locks and dams on one card, buddies who can ask about a day in the app, and a note on the White River rules that change November 1.

By Corey Boelkens · Changes through October 22, 2026 · RSS

What's new in the appThe numbers

Friday note

This week Pocket Fishing Guide learned the Arkansas River. Every lock and dam from Trimble to Norrell is on one strip, each pool reads the Corps every hour, and Ozark Lake has a page of its own beside Lake Dardanelle. If you fish a pool, save it and the latest read follows you.

Buddies grew up too. Open a buddy's calendar, tap a day, and the ask goes to them in the app with the read for your water beside it. They answer with one tap, and once you are both in, the day goes on your calendar.

On the White from Bull Shoals Dam down to the Norfork access, you can keep two rainbows a day now, and anything 14 inches or longer goes back. On November 1 the water just below the dam closes, and browns are catch-and-release through the state park. Check the water's page in the app for the rule as AGFC posts it.

Anglers who chose the Friday email in the app get this note there, with their own water's week, the dam's week by the hour and anything new since they last looked. This page is the public copy.

The regulation lines are from Corey's note in the Friday digest for 2026-10-23 (Pocket Fishing Guide), which quotes AGFC.

One ask this week

If you fish an Arkansas River pool, open its card and tell us whether the level and release match what you saw at the ramp. Something off? Tell us under the note in the app; the feedback carries the change and the build you are on.
