Market Overview & Executive Summary

Competitive scan as of June 23, 2026. Research sources: web search, app store intelligence, market reports.

HIGH PRIORITY: GilledIt emerging as category leader GilledIt launched a free-forever model with a built-in tackle marketplace (Stripe Connect, 8% fee) and algorithmic social feed — now ranked #1 fishing app for most anglers in 2026. Represents the strongest competitive threat to PFG's free-tier positioning.
OPPORTUNITY: Paywall backlash creating market opening Fishbrain ($10–13/month) and FishAngler (v5.0 paywall of core forecast features) are both seeing high user churn and negative reviews. Users are actively seeking free, feature-rich alternatives — PFG's timing is ideal.
ADVANTAGE: No competitor owns the Arkansas-specific niche Zero national fishing apps provide AGFC regulation-aware intelligence, Arkansas-specific water data, or 2026 trout limit changes. This local depth is PFG's strongest untouchable moat.

Lean Canvas — Pocket Fishing Guide

Business model snapshot as of June 2026, updated with competitive context.

Problem

  • No fishing app gives AR anglers hyperlocal intelligence without a paywall
  • National apps are shallow on Arkansas-specific waters, species, and regulations
  • Beginners have no structured path from "what do I fish?" to "how do I catch it?"
  • AGFC regulation changes (2026 trout limits) aren't surfaced in real time

Solution

  • 39 Arkansas water bodies with AGFC-aware, condition-tuned lure scoring
  • Real-time USGS, NWS, USACE data — no API keys, zero cost
  • 21-lure library ranked by clarity × flow × spawn phase
  • Pro Intel accordion with local expert knowledge per water body
  • Future: guide profiles, education paths, weekly digest

Unique Value Proposition

  • The only fishing guide built FOR Arkansas, BY Arkansas outdoors people
  • Free forever on core features — no subscription, no paywall
  • Field-tested on lakes & rivers that matter to AR anglers
  • “Pocket-sized expert” for every skill level from first cast to tournament

Unfair Advantage

  • AGFC relationship + local data no national app will replicate
  • TWT founder is an Arkansas angler — authentic credibility
  • Open source → community contributions & trust
  • Zero infrastructure cost = sustainable free tier indefinitely
  • AI-assisted development velocity (Claude, Codex, Copilot pipeline)

Customer Segments

  • Arkansas anglers (beginner → tournament)
  • Lake Maumelle crappie & white bass regulars
  • AGFC-licensed anglers (~600k AR fishing licenses)
  • AR fishing guides seeking booking & profile tools
  • Outdoor recreation families — first-time anglers

Key Metrics

  • DAU / WAU / MAU active users
  • Water selected events per session
  • Lure click-through rate
  • Return visit rate (funnel baseline)

Channels

  • Content: Founder / field time for water research & tip curation
  • Future: Supabase backend when user base justifies it

Revenue Streams

  • Patreon tier (future: free + premium + patron)

Strategic Opportunities

Ranked by impact × feasibility within PFG's current architecture and roadmap.

1

Capitalize on Fishbrain & FishAngler paywall backlash

Both leading apps have triggered high user churn with aggressive paywall moves. Users are explicitly naming free alternatives. A simple landing page message — "Free forever for Arkansas anglers. No subscriptions." — paired with an AGFC integration callout could convert defectors. Consider a targeted Arkansas fishing forum post.

Quick Win Growth
2

Own the 2026 AGFC regulation change narrative

The 2026 Arkansas Fishing Guidebook introduced new trout limits on Bull Shoals, Norfork, and Greers Ferry tailwaters due to 2025 stocking shortage. Lake Monticello also changed from catch-and-release to harvest. No fishing app surfaces these changes. PFG adding a "2026 Reg Update" chip or banner on affected waters is a zero-effort high-value differentiator that no national competitor can match quickly.

Quick Win Growth
3

Ship PWA + add-to-home-screen before v0.5.0

Every single competitor already has native app or PWA status. PFG is the only major fishing intelligence tool in the space that doesn't support add-to-home-screen. This is a critical gap for on-water use when users need offline or home-screen access. PWA manifest is already in the roadmap — prioritize it. It's the feature gap that makes PFG feel like a "website" vs. an "app" regardless of quality.

Quick Win Retention
4

Launch weekly Friday fishing digest via EmailJS

No competitor offers a free, state-specific weekly fishing digest. GilledIt and Fishbrain focus on social/in-app engagement loops. A simple EmailJS-powered Friday report with spawn phase, top water picks, and AGFC news for Arkansas creates a retention and distribution channel that competitors don't have and can't easily copy. Roadmap item — this should be a v0.5 priority given zero infrastructure cost.

Medium-term Retention Revenue Path
5

Build the beginner education gap nobody else is filling

Across all eight competitors analyzed, zero apps offer structured skill-level education (beginner → intermediate → advanced paths). The record 57.9M anglers in 2025 includes a massive wave of new-to-fishing users who don't know what a jig is. A "First Cast" or "Skill Level" toggle that surfaces simpler lures and technique explanations for beginners is a unique, defensible feature no national platform will build fast for a single state.

Medium-term Growth
6

Guide profiles + local bait shop directory as first revenue layer

Omnia Fishing's model (commerce integration with maps) validates that anglers accept commercial elements when they add value. PFG's Partner Program scoping (already documented) is the right move: local guide listings + nearby tackle shop directory per water body. Lake Maumelle guide listings would be a natural pilot. Omnia charges $59/yr — PFG's guide listing fee should be competitive at ~$49–99/yr per profile.

Medium-term Revenue
7

GilledIt's marketplace as benchmark — watch for AR guide monetization opportunity

GilledIt's Stripe Connect tackle marketplace (8% fee, escrow, offer negotiation) is the most innovative monetization move in the fishing app space in 2026. While PFG's scale doesn't justify a full marketplace yet, the model validates angler willingness to transact in-app. As PFG's guide profiles grow, in-app booking capability (guide trips, seminars) is a natural evolution. Monitor GilledIt's marketplace adoption as a bellwether.

Long-term Revenue
8

Claude API agentic features as v2.0 differentiator

Fishbox leads on AI fishing assistant; Fishbrain has BiteTime AI (paywalled). PFG's roadmap includes Claude API for pattern detection → auto-scoped issues and ML spawn prediction. When ready, a free AI fishing assistant that understands Arkansas water conditions, seasonal patterns, and AGFC regulations — something Fishbox cannot offer for Arkansas — would be a genuine breakthrough feature. The groundwork (AGFC data, spawn tracking, lure library) already exists.

Long-term Differentiation
