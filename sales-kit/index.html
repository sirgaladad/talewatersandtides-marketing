Where it stands, and what to fix first

The machine is built and loaded; nobody has been pitched yet. The first shop visit is the whole program's bottleneck.

Pricing rule. No number on any public surface, this page included. The offer lives on the invite and shows after "Yes, this is us" or a tied sign-in. You set it per business from the rate card in your wallet. Until the invite-offer slice ships, the console still prints old prices on /partners and in the PDF: show the draft page and the live examples, and do not open the plans block.

    What to say

    Never pitch the app; show their page. Pick the kind, then tap a line to copy it.

    The four-minute visit

    The 90-second call

    Tap to copy

    "Hi, is this [name]? Corey Boelkens. I guide the White out of Mountain Home. Quick one: I built the app Arkansas anglers use to check generation before they book, and I have already put a page together for [business] from what is on your site. It is free, and nothing is public until you say so. Can I text you the link so you can look at it when you are off the water? What is a good number?"

    Hang up. Text within five minutes. Do not explain pricing on the first call.

    The follow-up text

    Tap to copy

    Corey here from Pocket Fishing Guide. Here is the page I built for [business]: [invite link]. Your rates are on it as posted. Change anything, hit "Yes, this is us", and I will switch it on. Free. Call me with questions: [number].

    Who to talk to

    The owner or whoever answers the booking phone. At resorts the office manager books the guides (Gene's: ask for Scooter). Cotter Trout Lodge: Courtney Blank or Henry Seay. Never pitch a contractor guide at a dock without the dock's owner in the room; the dock collects the fee.

    What not to do

    Do not demo the game, the tank, Fish Buddy or the paddler feature. Do not say "AI". Do not quote visitor numbers unless asked, then quote them exactly. Do not discount; the lock is the offer. Never write a price on anything but the invite.

    Objections and the honest answer

    Answer with a fact you can show on the phone. If you cannot, say "not yet" and write it down; a "not yet" told straight is the premium signal.

    Raise these before they do: ownership changes (Gene's upper portion, Calico Rock "now locally owned", Beaver Dam Store). Ask who owns it today before you mention the draft. The memorial on Little Red Fly Fishing Trips: call first, ask who runs it.

    The pack

    Proof points and references

    Say only what is live or measured; name the window when you quote a number. Proof over adjectives is the brand.

    Claims you can make

    • 57 Arkansas waters, 33 species, every tailwater from Beaver to the Little Red.
    • Live USGS gauges, NWS forecast and the Corps or SWPA generation schedule on every water and partner page ("Water now").
    • Order on every list is river distance, then name. Sponsored adds placements; it never reorders a list.
    • Every door is counted first-party; no visitor is tracked; the business gets its own number weekly.
    • Facts come from the partner with a posted date; images carry the partner's licence; the house guide service is labelled and never sponsored.
    • About 500 visitors a month (Sep 19 to Oct 19): Bull Shoals 49, Norfork 24, Beaver 23. First 90 days (Feb to May 2026): 723 new users.
    • Species pages draw warm-water search: largemouth 32, channel cat 27, crappie 25 in 30 days.
    • Founding: first 25 Sponsored seats, price locked 24 months, through Mar 31 2027.
    • Terms v1: three-month minimum on monthly, 30 days' notice, 60 days' price notice, 30-day refund on a first term, 14-day lapse back to free. Nothing is deleted.

    Claims you cannot make

    • "Most-opened card in the app". Say "the card anglers check before they drive".
    • Booking, payments or a marketplace as live. They are next.
    • Email or text notifications as live. In-app today.
    • Stay or boat request rows. A lodge gets a text today.
    • "AI-powered" anything. Say "reads the gauges every 15 minutes".
    • Any number for a market other than North Central.
    • Any price, anywhere but the invite.

    The route

    North Central first: it holds the traffic, the four drafts and your boat. Heber Springs is an hour from Little Rock. Beaver is a phone-and-text market until Riverline ships.

    Paste into lead notes

    Build on site: Leads, convert, template, fix the facts with them, publish. Ten minutes each after the first two.

    The close, and after

    The close is a tied account and a published page, not a signature. Money is a second conversation, after they have seen their own Monday number.

    1. In the shop. "That's right": open the admin on your phone, tie the account to the email or phone you hold, clear kit.prospect as they confirm each fact, publish. Show them the screenshot.
    2. If they want to look first. Invite card with the code; text the link before you leave the lot; "Look yourself up" on /partners also finds the draft. Set the offer on the invite the same hour, from the rate card. Never improvised, never below the floor.
    3. The price reveal. "Yes, this is us" or a tied sign-in shows the offer card: their Sponsored price, locked 24 months as a founding partner, until a date. Until the slice ships, say it on the phone and write it in the lead note; nothing else carries the number.
    4. Day 1. Text the live page and the counter-card offer. Ask for a photo and a river note (20 to 280 characters, their words).
    5. Day 7 (Monday). Text the "This week" line by hand: seen, opened, each door by name. Every partner, every Monday, until weekly-snapshot ships.
    6. Day 14. Second Monday number plus the ask: "Your dam card slot is open on Bull Shoals. Your price is on your invite and it is locked two years. Want it?"
    7. Day 30. "What is wrong with the page?" Fix it the same day. That is the service.
    8. Each quarter. Numbers call with sponsors; one founding-partner story, with permission.

    Log every touch in the lead's note on the admin the same day. That note is the CRM until there is a CRM.

    Kill signals. Three unanswered texts over three weeks: archive with a note, never delete. A guide who asks you to remove rates: remove them, keep the page, add "Ask about a day".

    Four-week targets: 25 doors, 12 published, 4 sponsored, 100% Monday lines. Under 15 doors by Friday of week one, and the build block becomes a sell block.
