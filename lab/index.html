Pocket Fishing Guide

Live fishing intelligence for Arkansas anglers. Water temperature, barometric pressure and spawn phase across 57 waters (16 reservoirs, 9 tailwaters, 12 lakes, 16 rivers, 4 streams) and 33 species.
