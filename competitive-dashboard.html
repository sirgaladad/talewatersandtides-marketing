Deep profiles on key players — click details to expand full analysis.

onWater Fish

Funded startup · National · Founded ~2019
High Threat AI-Native Funded

First AI-native fishing app. Raised $7M total ($2M Dec 2025). 400K users across fishing + paddling apps. Projected $2.5M ARR by end 2026. Expanding to Canada. River-first database: 224K+ lakes, 201K+ rivers, 100K+ access points.

Angler Intelligence™ AI Chat Fish Measurement AI Smart Journal MyWaters Live River Gauges Hatch Forecasts Offline Charts Depth Layers (paid)
Full Analysis
  • Angler Intelligence™ (Oct 2025): Real-time AI advisor drawing from live river gauges, weather, regulations, access points, and historical patterns. Answers "when, what, where" in seconds.
  • Measure tool: Patented AI length/weight estimation from photos — refined across thousands of test cases.
  • Summer 2025 update: Smart journal + MyWaters personalized condition notifications when conditions match past successes.
  • Business model: Freemium — onWater+ unlocks AI chat, depth layers, offline mode. ~$3.99–$5.99/month.
  • PFG overlap: AI intelligence, spawn/condition alerts, educational content — all items in PFG's "Later" roadmap lanes. onWater is executing now.
  • PFG advantage: Arkansas hyperlocal depth (White Bass/Crappie spawn phases, 39 specific waters) vs. onWater's broad but shallow national coverage.

GilledIt

Independent · National · Rated #1 App 2026
Medium-High Threat Free Core

Ranked #1 fishing app for most anglers in 2026. Free core with fast catch logging. Only fishing app with built-in marketplace for buying/selling tackle. Photo-based fish ID powered by Fishial.AI arriving Q3 2026.

Catch Log (free) Tackle Marketplace Fish ID (Q3 2026) Species Database Community Feed
Full Analysis
  • Free-first model is resonating in 2026 — key differentiator vs. Fishbrain's hard paywall pivot.
  • Tackle marketplace is a novel monetization angle no other fishing app has shipped. Creates network effect and retention.
  • Fishial.AI fish ID (Q3 2026): Photo → species + size estimation. Adds educational value for beginners — directly relevant to PFG's skill-level expansion goal.
  • PFG overlap: Accessibility to beginner anglers; fish ID; community. Low overlap on real-time conditions or Arkansas-specific intelligence.
  • PFG advantage: PFG has condition-aware lure scoring, USGS live data, spawn phase intel — GilledIt is a log + marketplace, not an intelligence platform.

Fishbrain

VC-backed · Global · 20M users
Medium Threat Declining UX

Largest fishing app by users (20M worldwide). AI bite-time predictions using solunar data. However: maps, predictions, and catch location data all now locked behind Fishbrain Pro. Marketplace launched and abandoned. Growing user complaints about crashes and support.

AI Bite Predictions 20M Catch Database Maps (paid) Social Community Solunar Data
Full Analysis
  • Paywall aggression: Nearly all useful features now require Pro subscription. Community backlash is significant — Reddit and app store reviews trending negative in 2025–26.
  • Abandoned marketplace: Launched then quietly shut down — signals execution challenges and strategic drift.
  • User churn window: Frustrated Fishbrain users actively looking for alternatives. PFG + TWT's free, honest model is a direct answer.
  • PFG overlap: Bite timing, location data, social. Low on Arkansas-specific depth or real-time hydrology.
  • PFG advantage: Zero-cost, no paywalls, real conditions data vs. Fishbrain's paid prediction model.

AGFC Mobile

Arkansas Game & Fish Commission · State official app
Medium Threat Official

Official Arkansas hunting and fishing app. Licensing, game checks, interactive maps, public land/boat ramp info. Updated 2025 with improved game check offline submission. Not an intelligence platform — no condition-aware recommendations.

License Purchase Game Check Interactive Maps Regulations Offline Support
Full Analysis
  • The must-have compliance app for any Arkansas angler — nearly everyone who fishes AR has this installed.
  • Opportunity not threat: AGFC fills administrative needs; PFG fills intelligence needs. Natural complement, not competition.
  • Partnership angle: AGFC data (regulations, public access, water body metadata) could enhance PFG. Worth monitoring for API/data-sharing opportunities.
  • No spawn intel, no lure scoring, no real-time conditions analysis. PFG occupies a completely different use-case lane.

Lean Canvas — Pocket Fishing Guide

One-page business model snapshot as of Q2 2026. Based on current product state and roadmap priorities.

1. Problem
  • Arkansas anglers lack real-time, water-specific fishing intelligence
  • National apps (Fishbrain, onWater) are broad but shallow on AR waters
  • Existing resources are scattered: AGFC regs + YouTube tips + local knowledge
  • Beginners have no guided on-ramp; experienced anglers lack spawn precision
4. Solution
  • Free, static PWA — works on any phone, no login
  • 39 Arkansas water bodies with live USGS/NWS/USACE conditions
  • Condition-aware lure scoring for White Bass + Crappie
  • Spawn phase tracking with lure recommendations per phase
  • Pro Intel + Local Tips per water — expert knowledge packaged
3. Unique Value Proposition
"The only free fishing intelligence platform built specifically for Arkansas waters — no account, no paywall, real data, right now."
  • Depth-first, not breadth-first
  • Pocket = field-ready on the water, not at a desk
  • Zero friction: no login, no subscription, no ads
9. Unfair Advantage
  • Hyperlocal Arkansas knowledge embedded in product
  • Zero-cost infrastructure (GitHub Pages, static)
  • Community trust — built by Arkansas outdoorsmen for Arkansas anglers
  • USGS/NWS/USACE no-key data pipeline already built
  • AI-assisted development velocity (TWT Green/Red Team model)
2. Customer Segments
  • Primary: Recreational Arkansas anglers (White Bass, Crappie, casual)
  • Secondary: Beginners / new anglers entering the sport
  • Secondary: Tournament / serious anglers seeking edge
  • Future: Guides + outfitters seeking partner tools
  • Future: Paddlers (via Paddler expansion)
5. Channels
  • GitHub Pages (zero-cost hosting)
  • Organic search — AR fishing, spawn timing, lake conditions
  • Fishing communities (Reddit r/fishing, Facebook AR fishing groups)
  • Partner outreach — AGFC, tackle shops, guides
  • Email digest (planned — weekly spawn reports)
  • Social sharing — snapshot feature
6. Revenue Streams
  • Ko-fi donations (current — tip jar model)
  • Planned: Patreon tiers (free / premium / patron)
  • Planned: Partner / sponsor placements (local tackle shops)
  • Planned: Guide listings + promotion packages
  • Future: Premium API tier for outfitter integrations
7. Cost Structure
  • Infrastructure: $0 (GitHub Pages)
  • Data APIs: $0 (USGS, NWS, USACE — public)
  • Development: TWT team time (Corey + Abby + AI pipeline)
  • Future: Supabase / backend when user base justifies
  • Future: Claude API costs (agentic features)
8. Key Metrics
NOW TRACKING
  • GA4 active (GTM-M36K8X2Q)
  • Water selected events
  • Lure click-through rate
  • Tab engagement
NORTH STARS
  • Monthly active users
  • Return visit rate
  • Funnel: Load → Lure Clicked
  • p50 first-interaction < 3s
FUTURE
  • Catch report submissions
  • Email subscriber count
  • Patron conversion rate
  • ML spawn accuracy (±3 days)
Existing Alternatives (What users use today)
Fishbrain (paying) AGFC Mobile (compliance) Google search + YouTube Local fishing Facebook groups onWater (national) Local guide word-of-mouth

Strategic Opportunities

Ranked by impact × urgency. High = act this quarter. Medium = plan for next quarter. Low = monitor.

High Priority

Capture Fishbrain Churn

Fishbrain's hard paywall pivot is driving frustrated users out. They're actively searching for free alternatives. PFG's zero-paywall model is the direct answer.

→ Create SEO-optimized landing content: "Free Fishbrain alternative Arkansas", "Arkansas fishing app free". Share in AR fishing Facebook groups and Reddit.
High Priority

Ship PWA + Spawn Alerts Before onWater Deepens AR Coverage

onWater's MyWaters condition alerts are the exact feature PFG has on roadmap. Ship it first for AR waters — the spawn-window push notification is a sticky retention hook no competitor offers at zero cost.

→ Prioritize PWA manifest + browser push notifications in Q3 2026 roadmap lane. Arkansas-first, White Bass + Crappie spawn as first alert trigger.
High Priority

AGFC Partnership / Data Integration

AGFC is a complement, not competitor. Every AR angler has their app. A co-promotion, data sharing agreement, or even a featured listing on AGFC's digital channels would be extremely high-leverage reach.

→ Draft outreach to AGFC Digital/Communications team. Frame PFG as the intelligence layer to complement their compliance layer.
High Priority

Beginner On-Ramp Content (Education Layer)

GilledIt (Q3 2026 fish ID) and market data both signal beginner inclusivity as 2026's growth vector. PFG already has technique/lure content — package it as a guided "first trip" experience.

→ Add beginner mode / skill-level filter to Tackle Box. YouTube how-to links per technique (Maumelle deep dive lane) moves to higher priority.
Medium Priority

Weekly Email Digest — Pre-Season Launch

No competitor sends a free AR-specific weekly fishing report. The Friday digest (EmailJS on roadmap) is a direct retention + brand-building move that costs nearly zero to run with LLM generation.

→ Pilot a manual weekly email to early subscribers this fall. Use as training data for the automated LLM narrative report system planned for 2027.
Medium Priority

SEO — Build Discoverability Before AI Traffic Shifts

PFG is largely un-indexed by name. Search traffic for "Arkansas fishing conditions", "White Bass spawn 2026", "Maumelle crappie" represents high-intent organic traffic. Capture it now.

→ Add structured metadata, Open Graph tags, a sitemap. Write landing content per water body indexed for search. Zero-cost, high-return.
Medium Priority

Tackle Shop + Local Guide Partner Program

Local tackle shops and fishing guides are under-digitized in Arkansas. PFG's PARTNER-PROGRAM-SCOPING.md is already scoped — this is a revenue unlock and community anchor.

→ Execute on the scoped partner program. Start with 2–3 Maumelle-area shops as pilot partners. Use as case study for guide + outfitter expansion.
Medium Priority

Accelerate Claude API / Intelligence Layer

onWater has shipped AI intelligence. The window to be first in Arkansas with AI-native fishing advice is closing — but PFG's local data moat means a Claude-powered advisor here would dramatically outperform onWater's generic national answers for AR waters.

→ Spike a Claude API integration scoped to White Bass + Crappie on 5 core AR waters. Validate the data pipeline and UX pattern before full buildout.
Low Priority — Monitor

Bathymetry / Mapping Layer

SpotOn and onWater are betting on detailed maps. USGS bathymetry data is publicly available for AR waters. Low priority now, but a future differentiator if mapping demand proves sticky.

→ Monitor SpotOn growth. If user research surfaces map requests, explore USGS bathymetry tile integration as a Maumelle-first experiment.
Low Priority — Monitor

Catch Data Community + ML Training

Every competitor with community features is building proprietary catch data moats. PFG's planned catch reporting + Supabase backend is the right long-term play — but only after user base justifies backend costs.

→ Hold for "Backend & Scale" roadmap lane. Revisit when monthly active users cross 500+ sustained. Community catch data becomes ML training data for spawn prediction.
