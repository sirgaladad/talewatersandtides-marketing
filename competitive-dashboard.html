onWater Fish

Funded startup · National · Founded ~2019
High Threat AI-Native Funded

First AI-native fishing app. Raised $7M total ($2M Dec 2025). 400K users across fishing + paddling apps. Projected $2.5M ARR by end 2026. Expanding to Canada. River-first database: 224K+ lakes, 201K+ rivers, 100K+ access points.

Angler Intelligence™ AI Chat Fish Measurement AI Smart Journal MyWaters Live River Gauges Hatch Forecasts Offline Charts Depth Layers (paid)
Full Analysis
  • Angler Intelligence™ (Oct 2025): Real-time AI advisor drawing from live river gauges, weather, regulations, access points, and historical patterns. Answers "when, what, where" in seconds.
  • Measure tool: Patented AI length/weight estimation from photos — refined across thousands of test cases.
  • Summer 2025 update: Smart journal + MyWaters personalized condition notifications when conditions match past successes.
  • Business model: Freemium — onWater+ unlocks AI chat, depth layers, offline mode. ~$3.99–$5.99/month.
  • PFG overlap: AI intelligence, spawn/condition alerts, educational content — all items in PFG's "Later" roadmap lanes. onWater is executing now.
  • PFG advantage: Arkansas hyperlocal depth (White Bass/Crappie spawn phases, 39 specific waters) vs. onWater's broad but shallow national coverage.

AI Bite Predictions 20M Catch Database Maps (paid) Social Community Solunar Data

  • User churn window: Frustrated Fishbrain users actively looking for alternatives. PFG + TWT's free, honest model is a direct answer.
  • PFG overlap: Bite timing, location data, social. Low on Arkansas-specific depth or real-time hydrology.
  • PFG advantage: Zero-cost, no paywalls, real conditions data vs. Fishbrain's paid prediction model.

onX Fish
onX Inc. · National · Officially Launched April 2025
High Threat

Part of the onX outdoor ecosystem (onX Hunt, onX Offroad) which has a combined $270M valuation and 3M+ users. Lake Finder filters thousands of lakes by species, trophy/keeper potential, and scientific abundance data from state fish-and-wildlife agencies. Well-funded, cross-platform play.

Lake Finder State F&W Data Offline Maps Cross-App Ecosystem Trophy Scoring
Full Analysis
  • Ecosystem leverage: onX users who already pay for Hunt or Offroad can add Fish. Bundle model lowers acquisition cost — they don't need to win fishing users from scratch.
  • State F&W data integration: Lake Finder draws from state wildlife agency scientific data — abundance, trophy potential, species distributions. This is institutional data PFG doesn't yet have formal access to (AGFC partnership opportunity).
  • Not condition-aware: No real-time USGS/NWS integration confirmed. No spawn intelligence. Still broad and strategic rather than tactical-at-the-water.
  • Arkansas coverage: Limited at launch — focuses on Midwest waters. Arkansas-specific depth is not their moat. PFG's 39-water local intelligence remains unmatched here.
  • Threat vector: If onX adds real-time conditions or acquires a data partner, they close the gap rapidly. Watch for Arkansas expansion or AGFC data agreements.

FishRadar App
Independent · National · Updated Sept 2025
Medium — Watch

Partnered with marine sensor firms in September 2025 to integrate real-time water mapping, species forecasting, and AI bite probability analytics. Positions as sensor-aware intelligence platform — bridging hardware and software for on-water decision-making.

Marine Sensor Integration Real-Time Water Mapping AI Bite Probability Species Forecasting
Full Analysis
  • Sensor differentiation: Hardware integration (marine sensors → app) is a moat most pure-software competitors can't match. Similar positioning to Garmin's Navionics — but software-first.
  • AI bite probability: This is the feature set most directly competing with PFG's planned ML spawn prediction and lure scoring engine. The difference: PFG's model will be calibrated to AR waters; FishRadar is national.
  • Hardware dependency: Sensor integration raises the barrier for casual users — works against adoption for beginners and everyday anglers (PFG's sweet spot).
  • Watch signal: If FishRadar starts integrating public data feeds (USGS, NOAA) as a complement to sensor data, they could compete on PFG's core data moat.
AquaTrack Labs / HookMaster Digital
Acquired Aug 2025 · National · Recreational Analytics
Medium — Watch

AquaTrack Labs acquired HookMaster Digital in August 2025 to expand recreational fishing analytics. The merged platform integrates real-time fish activity forecasting, marine weather, geospatial catch logs, and community gamification. Signals consolidation trend — well-capitalized platforms absorbing niche tools.

Catch Log Analytics Weather Forecasting Gamification Charter Operator Tools Geospatial Logs
Full Analysis
  • Consolidation signal: This acquisition is the first confirmed M&A move in recreational fishing apps since the onWater raise. Suggests the market is maturing enough to attract roll-up capital.
  • Charter operator focus: Targeting guides and charter operators as a B2B layer — this is adjacent to PFG's planned guide/outfitter partner program. If AquaTrack locks up AR charter operators, it could block a PFG expansion vector.
  • Gamification: Social gamification + AR overlays drove 45% DAU increase in 2023. AquaTrack is leaning into this — beginner engagement mechanic PFG hasn't prioritized yet.
  • PFG action: Accelerate guide partner program outreach. Relationship with local AR guides before AquaTrack/HookMaster reaches them is a defensible first-mover move.
BassForce & BassForecast
Independent · Bass-Specific · AI-Native
Low-Medium — Niche

Two distinct bass-focused AI platforms. BassForce delivers real-time lure recommendations co-developed by pro anglers and AI. BassForecast generates a daily BFR (Bass Forecast Rating) score of 1–10 with push alerts for peak windows. Both validated: AI-native, species-specific intelligence is a viable model.

Pro Angler + AI Lure Recs Daily BFR Score Push Alerts Tournament Focus
Full Analysis
  • Market validation: BassForce and BassForecast confirm that anglers will pay for (and engage with) AI-native species-specific intelligence. This is PFG's planned roadmap — applied to White Bass and Crappie on Arkansas waters.
  • Differentiation: Both platforms are bass-only, tournament-oriented, and national. PFG's White Bass + Crappie + AR-local angle is a gap neither fills.
  • BFR model insight: A daily 1–10 "fishing score" with push alerts is a powerful retention mechanic. PFG's planned spawn-window push notifications are the closest equivalent — prioritize this feature.
  • Not a threat today: Audience segment doesn't overlap heavily with PFG's recreational + beginner focus. Becomes relevant if either platform expands to panfish or regional markets.

Lean Canvas — Pocket Fishing Guide

One-page business model snapshot as of Q2 2026. Based on current product state and roadmap priorities.

1. Problem
  • Arkansas anglers lack real-time, water-specific fishing intelligence
  • National apps (Fishbrain, onWater) are broad but shallow on AR waters
  • Existing resources are scattered: AGFC regs + YouTube tips + local knowledge
  • Beginners have no guided on-ramp; experienced anglers lack spawn precision
4. Solution
  • Free, static PWA — works on any phone, no login
  • 39 Arkansas water bodies with live USGS/NWS/USACE conditions
  • Condition-aware lure scoring for White Bass + Crappie
  • Spawn phase tracking with lure recommendations per phase
  • Pro Intel + Local Tips per water — expert knowledge packaged
3. Unique Value Proposition
"The only free fishing intelligence platform built specifically for Arkansas waters — no account, no paywall, real data, right now."
  • Depth-first, not breadth-first
  • Pocket = field-ready on the water, not at a desk
  • Zero friction: no login, no subscription, no ads
9. Unfair Advantage
  • Hyperlocal Arkansas knowledge embedded in product
  • Zero-cost infrastructure (GitHub Pages, static)
  • Community trust — built by Arkansas outdoorsmen for Arkansas anglers
  • USGS/NWS/USACE no-key data pipeline already built
  • AI-assisted development velocity (TWT Green/Red Team model)
2. Customer Segments
  • Primary: Recreational Arkansas anglers (White Bass, Crappie, casual)
  • Secondary: Beginners / new anglers entering the sport
  • Secondary: Tournament / serious anglers seeking edge
  • Future: Guides + outfitters seeking partner tools
  • Future: Paddlers (via Paddler expansion)
5. Channels
  • GitHub Pages (zero-cost hosting)
  • Organic search — AR fishing, spawn timing, lake conditions
  • Fishing communities (Reddit r/fishing, Facebook AR fishing groups)
  • Partner outreach — AGFC, tackle shops, guides
  • Email digest (planned — weekly spawn reports)
  • Social sharing — snapshot feature
6. Revenue Streams
  • Ko-fi donations (current — tip jar model)
  • Planned: Patreon tiers (free / premium / patron)
  • Planned: Partner / sponsor placements (local tackle shops)
  • Planned: Guide listings + promotion packages
  • Future: Premium API tier for outfitter integrations
7. Cost Structure
  • Infrastructure: $0 (GitHub Pages)
  • Data APIs: $0 (USGS, NWS, USACE — public)
  • Development: TWT team time (Corey + Abby + AI pipeline)
  • Future: Supabase / backend when user base justifies
  • Future: Claude API costs (agentic features)
8. Key Metrics
NOW TRACKING
  • GA4 active (GTM-M36K8X2Q)
  • Water selected events
  • Lure click-through rate
  • Tab engagement

  • Monthly active users
  • Return visit rate
  • Funnel: Load → Lure Clicked
  • p50 first-interaction < 3s
FUTURE
  • Catch report submissions
  • Email subscriber count
  • Patron conversion rate
  • ML spawn accuracy (±3 days)
