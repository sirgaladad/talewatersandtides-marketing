What you get

A 90-day engagement, week by week.

For a shop, clinic, salon, or service business with 5–50 people. We pick two workflows that eat your week and make AI handle the first draft of each.

  1. Weeks 1–2Assess

    Find the two workflows

    • Half-day on site watching how work moves
    • Inventory of tools you already pay for
    • Pick two workflows: usually inquiries and follow-up

    You get: one-page plan with the two workflows, the tools, and what stays manual

  2. Weeks 3–4Implement

    Build it in your tools

    • Draft-and-approve set up in your booking, email, or CRM
    • A person named as owner for each workflow
    • Nothing goes out without a human yes

    You get: both workflows live, in test mode, with the owner trained to approve

  3. Weeks 5–6Train

    Train on real work

    • Two 90-minute sessions with your team, on their own inbox
    • Cheat sheets per role
    • Switch from test to live

    You get: team running it; one-page cheat sheet per role

  4. Weeks 7–10Run

    Run it with you

    • Weekly review of what AI drafted and what got changed
    • Fix the prompts, not the people
    • Add guardrails where it drifted

    You get: the runbook: how it works, who owns it, what to do when it breaks

  5. Weeks 11–13Improve

    Read the numbers

    • Hours saved, response time, misses
    • Decide: stop, keep, or add a third workflow
    • Hand-off or monthly tune-up

    You get: day-90 numbers and a recommendation in plain language

Your time

2–3 hours a week

Mostly weeks 1–2 and 5–6. Less once it runs.

Cadence

Weekly 45-minute check-in

Same slot every week. Slack or text in between.

After day 90

Monthly tune-up or you run it

The runbook is yours either way.

For a marketing or operations leader with a team and a stack. I join as a fractional AI and MarTech lead for a quarter, embedded in your week.

  1. Weeks 1–2Assess

    Audit stack and workload

    • Interview the team; map where hours go
    • Audit CRM, marketing automation, data flows
    • Shortlist five use cases, rank by payoff vs. risk

    You get: stack map and a ranked use-case list with owners

  2. Weeks 3–5Implement

    Ship the first two

    • Build inside your CRM and automation tools
    • Data hygiene fixes that block the rest
    • Review gates: human approval before anything reaches a customer

    You get: two use cases in production with approval gates and logging

  3. Weeks 6–7Train

    Enable the team

    • Role-based sessions on the team's live work
    • Prompt library in a shared doc
    • Office hours twice a week

    You get: prompt library, role guides, office-hours cadence

  4. Weeks 8–11Run

    Operate and extend

    • Weekly ops review inside your existing meeting
    • Use cases three and four
    • Vendor and cost review

    You get: runbooks for every live use case; vendor recommendation

  5. Weeks 12–13Improve

    Report to leadership

    • Hours, throughput, quality, cost
    • Roadmap for next quarter
    • Decide: extend, hand off, or hire

    You get: leadership readout and a next-quarter roadmap

Questions

Straight answers.

What does Tale Waters and Tides do?

AI implementation for small businesses: setup inside the tools you already pay for, training on your team's real work, and support until it sticks. Also fractional AI and MarTech leadership for marketing and operations teams.

How long is an engagement?

90 days, in five stages: assess, implement, train, run, improve. Every engagement ends with a runbook your team can run without us.

Do we need to buy new software?

Usually not. We build inside the email, booking, CRM and office tools you already pay for. New tools come last, not first.
