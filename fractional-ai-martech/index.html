Questions

Straight answers.

What does it cost?

One quote for the quarter, set after a stack conversation, with the use-case count and cadence written into it. It renews quarter to quarter or hands off to a hire; I will help write the job description.

Who is this for?

Marketing and operations leaders who have a team and a stack: a CRM, marketing automation, data flowing between them, and people whose hours go into the gaps. If you have neither a team nor a stack yet, the 90-day setup is the better door.

How is this different from an agency or a consultant deck?

I work inside your meetings and your tools, with the people doing the work. The deliverables are running use cases with approval gates and logging, not a slide deck. The readout to leadership comes at the end, from the numbers.

How do you measure the quarter?

Four numbers per use case, baselined in weeks 1 to 2 and reported in week 13: hours, throughput, quality, and cost. Vendor and tool cost is reviewed in weeks 8 to 11 and goes in the same report.

Which tools do you work in?

HubSpot, Salesforce and Zoho; Mailchimp, Klaviyo and Constant Contact; Google Workspace and Microsoft 365; Slack and Teams; Snowflake and the data flows between them. New tools are proposed in the readout, not bought in week 3.

Can this turn into a full-time or executive role?

Yes. Corey is open to fractional and executive roles in marketing technology, analytics and applied AI. The quarter is a working interview in both directions.
